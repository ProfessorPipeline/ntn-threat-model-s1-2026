## Independent scoring

Target: 10–15 shared threat records using the latest F and I criteria.

Starting data: five supplied scored examples and two distinct unscored plan drafts. At least three more suitable candidates are needed for ten. Do not split duplicates just to reach a target.

2. Each student submits initial ratings in Independent scoring without discussion.
3. Initial ratings lock. The comparison appears after both submit for that record revision.
4. Count agreement for F and I separately.
5. Discuss every difference against the written criteria.
6. Record an agreed score with its reason and deciding criterion or mark it Escalated.
7. Export the evaluation after both finish.
